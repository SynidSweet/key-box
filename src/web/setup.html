Set up the key box

This phone becomes the one that approves key actions. It makes a passkey and a device key; both are needed for every approval.

This phone keeps a device key that cannot be copied. If you reinstall the app or clear its data, you will need to recover.

The app was reinstalled or its data cleared: recover with the authenticator app on the same phone.

The phone is lost: recover with the authenticator app restored from your Google account on a new phone.

The box sends its traffic only to the services it needs and refuses the rest. These limits do not stop an approved update that is malicious.
