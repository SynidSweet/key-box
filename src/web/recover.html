Recover or add a phone

With the twelve words from the box's console, this phone becomes the only approver and every other phone stops approving.

With the words an approving phone was shown when it added a phone, this phone joins as one more approver.

The app was reinstalled or its data cleared: recover with the authenticator app on the same phone.

The phone is lost: recover with the authenticator app restored from your Google account on a new phone.
